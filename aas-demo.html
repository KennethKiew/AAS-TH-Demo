Our Story

Three Decades of Precision

Founded in Bangkok in 1988, AAS began as a single Porsche dealership with a singular conviction: that the ownership of a great machine should be accompanied by an equally great relationship. That conviction has since grown to encompass four of the world's most iconic brands, served across Thailand by a team of over 800 dedicated professionals.

We don't simply sell vehicles. We initiate a lifelong relationship between owner and machine — from the moment of selection to decades of meticulous care.
